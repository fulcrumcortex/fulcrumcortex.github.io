Design Sidekick

Give the model a clear brief.

Prepare a constrained spatial brief and, when connected, review a concept in the editable CAD model.

What it can help you do

  • Separate supplied dimensions from assumptions.
  • Plan editable building elements and circulation.
  • Review plan, 3D and section consistency.

Try a focused request

Prepare a compact one-bedroom cabin concept brief with dimensions and unresolved site assumptions.

Skills behind the role

Prepare an editable CAD design brief

Translate a spatial design request into a constrained Fulcrum CAD brief and reviewable modelling steps. Use for concept design, not engineering sign-off or construction certification.

Expected output: A constrained modelling brief or verified editable concept, with assumptions kept visible.

Access and verification
